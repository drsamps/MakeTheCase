# Welcome to Make The Case

**Make The Case** facilitates enhanced student learning by allowing students to engage in AI-simulated conversations about relevant course topics as they apply to business situations. Students chat with AI-driven case protagonists (e.g., a CEO) and practice making and defending decisions in realistic scenarios.

## A typical instructor workflow

- **Install cases that are already written.** Use cases from the shared library, or import your own.
- **Create new cases using [Case Writer](#/case-writer).** Case Writer is an AI-assisted authoring tool that turns a teaching principle and source material into a published case and scenario.
- **Set up scenarios that students will address in a case chat.** A *scenario* defines the situation a student will engage with, including the protagonist, the question the student must answer, and (optionally) the positions they can take.
- **Set up case chats with various options**, including:
  - the scenarios that will be used
  - the personality of the conversation (chatbot personas)
  - whether and how many hints are available
  - whether students see the case document
  - whether their work is evaluated automatically
- **Assign the case chat to students** in your sections.
- **Evaluate the results** in preparation for class discussion — review transcripts, evaluations, and student positions.

## Where to start

- **Setup → Personas** lets you preview and customize the protagonist personalities.
- **Content → Cases** is where cases and case files live.
- **Courses → Sections** is where you organize semesters, courses, and student rosters.
- **Assignments** is where you publish a case to a section with specific options.
- **Monitor** lets you watch chats live and follow student progress.
- **Results** lets you review completed chats and evaluations.

When you're ready to see what's happening right now, switch to the **Dashboard** sub-tab above.
