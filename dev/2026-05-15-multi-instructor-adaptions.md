# Multi-Instructor MakeTheCase and Case Writer

I would like the MakeTheCase and Case Writer app to be multi-instructor where each instructor has their own resources separate from other instructor’s resources. Each instructor has their own:

* Courses  
* Course Sections with Students  
* Cases  
* Case Assignments  
* Rubrics  
* AI Model API Keys  
* Other?

Each instructor can be part of an Instructor Team who can share:

* Cases  
* Rubrics

Instructors are assigned to Courses and can:

* create and manage Course Sections  
* manage Students and Chat Assignments within their Course Sections  
* view case chat Results from their Course Sections

TAs (Teaching Assistants) are assigned to specific course Sections and can:

* manage Students and Chat Assignments within their assigned Sections  
* view and manage case chat Results from their assigned Sections

Only an Admin can access functions under the Admin top-level menu, including:

* Instructors (and TAs who act as Section-specific instructors)  
* Settings  
* Models  
* Personas  
* Prompts  
* Admins  
* Logging

In addition, only Admins can:

* manage Semesters, including  
  * creating New Semester  
  * setting a semester as Current  
* create Courses  
* assign Instructors to Courses

It might be good to flag Admin-only items on the Instructor Dashboard with some symbol like an asterisk, so that the Admin will know what only he can see.

What are other issues to consider in making MakeTheCase (and Case Writer) handle multiple instructors? Ask me relevant questions as we develop a comprehensive plan.
